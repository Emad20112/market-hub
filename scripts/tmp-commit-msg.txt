fix(catalogue): reconcile the index with the data it actually holds

A full audit (scripts/audit-catalogue.mjs) found 22 inconsistencies and
one integrity break. All are fixed and the audit now reports zero.

Data
- Two items had lost their SKU when their twins were deleted, leaving
  them nameless in every lookup; RM-WHEAT-HARD and SRV-MILL-TON restored.
- A packaging screen wrote the Arabic name into the English column, which
  is why every item listed its name twice. It also wrote a column that
  does not exist and created stock items with no unit, so the balance had
  no meaning and no movement could be posted against them.
- Bags were named "flour bags"; they serve every product the mill makes.
- A 50kg sack was called a bag in English and a sack in Arabic. It is a
  sack. Bag/sack/kilogram/ton/piece now have one Arabic symbol each
  (ك / ش / كجم / طن / ق), and the unused 40kg bag is gone.
- A milling service was purchasable; services are sold, not bought.
- An empty category removed itself from the index.

Settings
- Five ready-made profiles (spare parts, grocery, retail, mill, custom)
  are gone. They promised dimensions this catalogue does not have, and
  the dialog offered toggles for tables that hold nothing. Three real
  dimensions remain, and a legacy stored setting can no longer switch
  the closed ones back on.

Screens
- The packaging screen no longer asserts a fixed margin and sack price;
  both are computed from the items on screen.
- Category and unit are read separately from brand, so an unreadable
  optional relation can no longer blank them together.

Still outstanding, and deliberately not touched: one 5 r.y manual line
on a test invoice whose product was deleted. It is a record, not a
defect, so it needs an owner's decision.
